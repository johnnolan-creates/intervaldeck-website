3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Mobile & Wear OS App (IntervalDeck — Flow Timers)

IntervalDeck is designed to work offline. We do not require account registration and do not collect your name, email address, or personal profile inside the App.

  • Local App Data: Your custom interval timers, playlists, settings, and purchase status cache are stored locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Crash Logs and Diagnostics: If the App crashes or encounters an error, anonymous diagnostic data (such as stack traces, device model, and Android OS version) may be transmitted via Google Play / Android system diagnostics to help identify and fix bugs. These crash logs do not contain personally identifiable information (PII).
    • Lawful Basis (UK GDPR): Legitimate interests (maintaining app stability, security, and performance).
  • In-App Purchases: Optional upgrades (such as unlocking the Supporter tier) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your payment card details or billing address. Google provides us only with non-sensitive transaction tokens and purchase status confirmations to unlock features and restore purchases.
    • Lawful Basis (UK GDPR): Performance of a contract.

B. Website Mailing List (IntervalDeck.com)

  • Email Address: If you voluntarily subscribe to our mailing list on IntervalDeck.com, we collect your email address to send product updates, release news, and announcements.
    • Lawful Basis (UK GDPR): Consent (which you may withdraw at any time).

4. Device Permissions

The App requests only the device permissions necessary for core timer functionality:

  • Notifications: To display active timer progress, alerts, and completion notifications.
  • Vibration: To provide haptic alerts when intervals start, transition, or complete.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) / Audio: To announce interval names, countdowns, and cues aloud during hands-free sessions.
  • Background Execution / Foreground Service: To keep timers running accurately when your screen is locked or while you use other apps.

These permissions operate locally on your device and are never used to record audio, track your activity, or transmit personal data.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited technical or subscription data only with trusted third-party processors required to operate the App and Website:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing (Google LLC): Processes in-app purchases and receives anonymous crash logs/diagnostics. View Google's Privacy Policy.
  • MailerLite: Manages our Website newsletter subscriptions and delivers emails to opted-in subscribers. View MailerLite's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Hosts our static Website (intervaldeck.com). GitHub may collect standard server access logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability. View GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

  • Security: All data transmitted between your device, Google Play, and MailerLite is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Local App Data Retention: Local timer data remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
  • Crash Logs Retention: Diagnostic logs retained within Google Play Console are automatically purged according to Google's standard retention schedules.
